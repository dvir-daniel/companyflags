# Code of conduct

CompanyFlags is built in the open, by people with different backgrounds, experience and ways of working. We want
taking part to be a good experience for every one of them.

We follow the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).
It applies in every project space: issues, pull requests, discussions, reviews, chats and events, and wherever someone
represents the project.

## What we expect

- Be kind and patient, especially with people new to the project or to open source.
- Assume good intent. Ask before you conclude.
- Criticize ideas and code, never people.
- Accept a maintainer's decision on scope gracefully, and say so plainly when you disagree.

## What isn't acceptable

Harassment of any kind; insults, personal or political attacks; sexualized language or imagery; publishing someone's
private information; and anything else a reasonable person would consider inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Reporting

Tell the maintainers privately: send a message to any maintainer listed on the repository, or open a private report
through **Security → Report a vulnerability** if nothing else works for you. We read every report, keep the
reporter's identity private, and answer within a week.

Maintainers who don't follow this code in good faith may be removed from the project.
