# Better Auth is an adapter behind people and access

Who may join was decided in three places (Better Auth's hooks in `betterAuth.ts`, invites issued in `control.ts`, the first-account rule in `people.ts`), and adding single sign-on spread it further: SSO rules in the hooks, `identity.ts` reading `sso_provider`, and the `Identity` type carrying Better Auth so `api.ts` could hand it to `sso.ts`. We put people, invites, every admission rule, the role checks and the remove-access cascade in one module, `server/access.ts`, with Better Auth and its SSO plugin as the adapter behind it: their hooks ask the access module, and only that adapter reads or deletes Better Auth's `auth_*` rows. Who may join is now decided in one place and tested at that module's interface without Better Auth, which overturns the split stated in `betterAuth.ts`'s old header comment (Better Auth decides who may have an account, `people.ts` what they may do).
